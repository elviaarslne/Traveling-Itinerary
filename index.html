Trip Summary Roadmap

  • Nov 8 Warm-up hike at Gunung Pancar (Sentul)
  • Nov 9 Scenic nature walk at Enchanting Valley (Puncak)
  • Nov 10 Evening departure via KA Gajayana from Gambir to Malang
  • Nov 11 Arrive in Malang; Kajoetangan Heritage Village walk
  • Nov 12 Batu highland waterfalls (Coban Rondo) & orchards

Sunday, Nov 8

Gunung Pancar Warm-Up Hike

Shaded pine forest trails and natural sulfur hot springs in Sentul. Moderate cardio conditioning.

Monday, Nov 9

Enchanting Valley (Puncak)

Scenic highland nature walk near Taman Safari with mountain views and photography spots.

Tuesday, Nov 10 (Evening)

Departure to Malang

Arrive at Gambir by 17:50. Board KA Gajayana (#36) at 18:50. Overnight train journey.

Wednesday, Nov 11

Arrival & Kajoetangan Heritage Village

Arrive at Malang Kotabaru at 07:06. Hotel check-in, rest, and afternoon walk through Dutch-colonial vintage alleyways.

Thursday, Nov 12

Batu Highlands & Coban Rondo Waterfall

Private day driver to Coban Rondo waterfall trail, apple orchards, and a cozy mountain-view cafe.

Friday, Nov 13

Jeep-Only Mount Bromo Sunrise

01:30 AM Jeep pickup. Sunrise at King Kong Hill, Sea of Sand traverse (skipping strenuous steps), and Whispering Savannah.

Saturday, Nov 14

Souvenirs & Executive Return Train

Relaxed morning, local shopping, and afternoon train back to Jakarta.

Sunday, Nov 15

Arrival Home

Arrive back in Jakarta at dawn to unpack and rest.
